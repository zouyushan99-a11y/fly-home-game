# Educational content and original-source audit

Brief: user-provided “URBAN WINGS – IMPROVE THE EDUCATIONAL KNOWLEDGE POPUPS, LEARNING EXPERIENCE AND FINAL KNOWLEDGE SUMMARY.pdf”. This change improves the existing Fly Home implementation; it does not rebrand or rebuild the game.

The six bilingual topics integrate the supplied brief with existing game explanations. English main text contains 56–63 words per topic, divided into impact, cause and solution. Species/context qualifications are preserved. Fishpond content describes a general explanatory scene, never claims the selected route rendered it. Health, Energy and drift are explicitly labelled game representations.

All six SVG diagrams are original vector artwork generated by `tools/build-education-art.py`. No external stock images or additional licensed media were introduced. Existing game sky/building assets are unchanged. `topics.json` is the editable content source; `tools/sync-education.py` embeds content and images, and `tools/sync-building-art.py` refreshes the offline edition.

## Original sources requested by the brief

1. [HKBWS – Hong Kong Sparrow Census 2025](https://cms.hkbws.org.hk/cms/en/hkbws/work/resarch/sparrow-tw/sparrowcensus2025-eng)
2. [HKBWS – Mai Po Inner Deep Bay Ramsar Site Waterbird Monitoring Programme 2016–17](https://www.hkbws.org.hk/web/chi/documents/report/summer_report_2016.pdf)
3. [HKBWS – International Black-faced Spoonbill Census 2025](https://cms.hkbws.org.hk/cms/en/hkbws/work/endangered-species/bfs-en/bfs-census-2025-en)

Verification attempted during this work: each HTTPS request was rejected by the execution environment's network proxy with `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`. This is not evidence that HKBWS itself returned an error or that the links are invalid. No original report content was retrieved. Reference titles and URLs come from the user's brief and are explicitly presented as awaiting verification.

Accordingly **no statistical numbers from these sources are displayed as verified**. This includes the brief's sparrow estimate, volunteer/route/district counts, spoonbill regional/global counts and changes, and the two suggested historical waterbird figures. The optional AFCD figure is omitted as well. No overseas statistics, independently inferred counts or causal claims were substituted.

The final screen preserves the three actual original-source hyperlinks, explains differing methods and scopes, and clearly states that statistics are omitted pending original-source verification. It includes the requested citizen-science note. Future data cards require direct review of the original report, including geography, date, method, estimate/count classification and a matching source link; changing the audit status alone is insufficient.
